research.adetayo.dev: three directions

A scroll-through website for the HFT manipulation paper: full paper text, lots of plain-language explanation and animation (a phone where ntfy alerts pile up exactly as the watcher sends them). Light theme, precise-technical plus calm-editorial, English/French toggle. · Scroll-driven explainer

Pick a direction (A, B or C), or mix ingredients across them. Swap any component by its code, for example "B, but use S3 instead of B2". Every direction is a blend: the final build combines the ideas into one original design.
A

Margin Notes

The paper set like a journal article, with a live margin: plain-language notes, animated figures and the phone sit beside the text they explain.

It's the site where the paper reads like a printed article, and the margin comes alive: the phone in the margin buzzes with the detector's real alerts as you read the results.

theme: Lighttype: Display Zodiak / Text Sentient (serif) / Data JetBrains Monomotion: Signature: the phone pinned in the margin through Section 6 replays a real session (8 Oct) minute by minute as you scroll: amber early warnings drop in, 5 minutes later each flips to a silent green 'cleared' or a red 'confirmed'. Scroll: margin figures draw as their paragraph enters. Interaction: hover a citation for the source; drag the alert dial. Ambient: faint graph-paper grid drifting in the hero only.

B

Daylight Terminal

A trading terminal redrawn for daylight: hairline grid, monospace labels, every claim shown as a live chart you can poke.

It's the site where you watch the Isolation Forest cut a chart apart until one suspicious minute is left alone, then your phone buzzes.

theme: Lighttype: Display Supreme / Data Geist Monomotion: Signature: hero draws a real minute chart, then random cuts slice the feature space until one point is isolated in three cuts. Scroll: ticker tape of the 20 stocks; candles push and reverse for momentum ignition. Interaction: a threshold dial (90/95/99 gate, cut-off 40/50/60) that changes alerts per day live. Ambient: grid cells light under the pointer.

Plain Version

Pudding-style scrollytelling: a sticky stage plays the illustration while text steps scroll past, and every section can flip between the paper and a plain-language version.

It's the site where every paragraph has a 'say it simply' switch, and the pictures change as you scroll.

theme: Lighttype: Display Gambarino / Text Satoshimotion: Signature: the Paper / Plain switch morphs a section's text in place. Scroll: sticky stage swaps scenes per step (order book, spoofing wall, phone). Interaction: explainer accordions under each section. Ambient: a soft flicker grid in the section tints.
